# About ReadTheMasters

> <span class="pop" role="button" tabindex="0" style="cursor:help" aria-label="Show the source of the Abel quotation"><em>It seems to me that if one wants to make progress in mathematics, one should study the masters, not the pupils.</em> — Niels Henrik Abel<span class="pop-content" role="note"><em>Mémoires de Mathématiques par N. H. Abel</em>, Paris, 9 August 1826, in the margin of p. 79 (Ms.fol.351 A, National Library of Norway).<br><br>Original French: <em>Au reste il me paraît que si l'on veut faire des progrès dans les mathématiques il faut étudier les maîtres et non pas les écoliers.</em></span></span>

ReadTheMasters publishes **AI transcriptions and translations** of landmark public-domain
mathematics and physics texts, rendered as clean LaTeX, with the original source always cited.

Our goals:

- Revive historic works as faithful, reusable LaTeX transcriptions and readable translations.
- Keep the whole corpus **open (CC0)**.

We publish **only public-domain texts** — verified by an automated, sourced copyright check that
applies both the author's-death rule (life + 70) and the US 95-year publication rule.

Every text carries a **quality status** — from *AI draft* (machine output, not yet human-checked)
through *skimmed* (a quick pass) to *verified* (a full, rigorous check against the source) — and
records which AI model produced it. Nothing hides how it was made.

The project is community-powered: contributors make their own transcriptions and translations and
submit them for review. See **[Contribute](/contribute/)**.
